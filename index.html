High-latitude rule

Only matters far north or south (Scandinavia, northern Canada, etc.), where summer nights get too short for Fajr/Isha to calculate normally. Leave on Default otherwise.

Hijri date adjustment (days)

Needs 🔔 turned on (top-right) and only fires while this tab is open. On phones it has to be the foreground tab, since mobile browsers suspend background tabs and nothing fires while you're in another app or the screen's off. On desktop the tab can sit unfocused or minimized in the background and will still alert.

  • Azan, Jamat & LeaveAzan is the call to prayer, tap its row to adjust it if your mosque's clock differs from the calculated time. Jamat is when congregation starts, azan plus the +Nm gap shown on each row (tap it to edit both). Leave is the big time shown: jamat minus your travel time, so leaving then gets you there right as jamat starts.
  • Row colors Green, azan has started, no rush yet.
    Red, time to leave, or jamat has started.
    Amber, jamat's window has passed but a second jamat is often still going nearby for another 15 min (not shown for Jumu'ah).
    Grey, this prayer's window has fully passed.
  • 🔊 Test soundPlays the alert tone on demand, also unlocks audio playback in your browser so the real alert can play later.
  • SunriseShown under Fajr, for reference.
  • Jumu'ah on FridaysOn Fridays, Dhuhr is replaced by Jumu'ah with its own azan-to-jamat gap (tap it to edit) to account for the khutbah. Its window just ends when jamat passes, no second-jamat amber.
  • Location & favoritesTap the location name (top left) to search any city, use your current location, or star it to save as a favorite for quick switching later.
  • ⚙️ SettingsCalculation method, high-latitude rule (for far-north/south locations), Hijri date adjustment, and travel time to the mosque all live here.
  • Install the appUse your browser's "Install app" option (desktop) or "Add to Home Screen" (mobile) to add Namaz Times as its own icon with offline support.
