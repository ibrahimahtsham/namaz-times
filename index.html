How to use
  • Azan & JamatAzan is the call to prayer; Jamat is when the congregation actually starts. The +Nm tag on each row is the gap between them — tap it to edit if your mosque's timing differs.
  • Row colors Green — azan has started, still time to go pray.
    Red — jamat is starting right now.
    Amber (Fajr only) — jamat has passed but you can still pray individually until sunrise.
    Grey — this prayer's time has fully passed.
  • 🔊 Test soundPlays the alert tone on demand — also unlocks audio playback in your browser so the real alert can play later.
  • 🔕 / 🔔 NotificationsTap to allow (or mute) a browser notification each time a prayer's azan begins, in addition to the sound.
  • Location & methodTap the location name (top left) to search any city, use your current location, or change the calculation method.
  • Install the appUse your browser's "Install app" option (desktop) or "Add to Home Screen" (mobile) to add Namaz Times as its own icon with offline support.
